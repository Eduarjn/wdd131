SIP Sizer

Site name

SIP Sizer

SIP is the protocol that carries voice calls over the internet, and sizer states plainly what the site does: it tells you how much you need to buy. The two words together read as one idea, they are easy to say out loud on a call with a client, and they keep the site findable by the term the target audience already searches for.

Possible domain: sip-sizer.com

Site purpose

SIP Sizer helps small and mid–size businesses plan a VoIP phone system before they sign a contract with a carrier. Many companies buy more SIP trunks than they will ever use and overpay every month, while others buy too few and drop calls at peak hours. The site provides three services:

  • A trunk calculator that turns the number of agents, calls per hour and average call duration into a recommended number of simultaneous channels.
  • A bandwidth estimator that shows how much internet capacity those calls will consume with each common audio codec.
  • A glossary of telecom terms, so a business owner who is not an engineer can read a carrier proposal and understand it.

A quote request form lets a visitor send the result of a calculation to an integrator and receive a formal proposal.

Scenarios

These are the questions a visitor arrives with. The content of the site exists to answer them.

  • How many simultaneous SIP channels does a call center with 25 agents need?
  • How much internet bandwidth will 30 concurrent calls consume if I use the G.729 codec?
  • My carrier proposal says DID, PABX and Erlang. What do those terms mean?
  • How do I send my sizing result to an integrator so they can quote it?

Color schema

Five colors, chosen to read as telecom infrastructure rather than consumer software: a deep navy for structure, a signal green that the audience already associates with a call in progress, and an amber used only on the dark surfaces so the contrast stays safe.

Typography

Three typefaces, each with one job.

Trunk sizing for 25 agents

Roboto Slab, bold. Headings only – h1, h2 and h3. The slab serifs give the page a technical, instrument–panel weight.

Every paragraph, label and navigation link.

Roboto, regular and bold. Body copy, navigation, form labels and buttons. It stays readable at small sizes on a phone.

18 channels · 1.37 Mbps · G.729

Roboto Mono, regular. Calculator output, hex color codes and any figure the visitor may want to copy. Fixed width keeps columns of numbers aligned.

Wireframes

Home page layout in two viewports. The calculator is the reason people come, so it sits above the fold in both. On mobile the form fields and the result stack in one column; on wider screens the result panel moves beside the form, so a visitor can change an input and watch the number update without scrolling.
