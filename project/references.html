References

Sources for the figures and formulas used on SIP Sizer.

Traffic engineering

  • Erlang, A. K. (1917). Solution of some problems in the theory of probabilities of significance in automatic telephone exchanges. The Post Office Electrical Engineers' Journal. Origin of the Erlang B formula used by the trunk calculator.
  • International Telecommunication Union. ITU-T Recommendation E.500: Traffic intensity measurement principles. itu.int/rec/T-REC-E.500. Definition of the busy hour.

Codecs and bandwidth

  • Cisco Systems. Voice over IP – Per Call Bandwidth Consumption. cisco.com. Source of the 40 byte IP, UDP and RTP header figure and the per call bandwidth method used in the codec table.
  • International Telecommunication Union. ITU-T Recommendation G.711: Pulse code modulation (PCM) of voice frequencies. itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.711.
  • International Telecommunication Union. ITU-T Recommendation G.722: 7 kHz audio-coding within 64 kbit/s. itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.722.
  • International Telecommunication Union. ITU-T Recommendation G.726: 40, 32, 24, 16 kbit/s adaptive differential pulse code modulation. itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.726.
  • International Telecommunication Union. ITU-T Recommendation G.729: Coding of speech at 8 kbit/s. itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.729.
  • Valin, J-M., Vos, K., and Terriberry, T. (2012). RFC 6716: Definition of the Opus Audio Codec. IETF. rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6716.
  • Andersen, S. et al. (2004). RFC 3951: Internet Low Bit Rate Codec (iLBC). IETF. rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3951.

Protocols

  • Rosenberg, J. et al. (2002). RFC 3261: SIP – Session Initiation Protocol. IETF. rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3261.
  • Schulzrinne, H. et al. (2003). RFC 3550: RTP – A Transport Protocol for Real-Time Applications. IETF. rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3550.
  • International Telecommunication Union. ITU-T Recommendation E.164: The international public telecommunication numbering plan. itu.int/rec/T-REC-E.164.

Call quality

  • International Telecommunication Union. ITU-T Recommendation G.114: One-way transmission time. itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.114. Source of the 150 millisecond latency guidance in the glossary.
  • International Telecommunication Union. ITU-T Recommendation P.800: Methods for subjective determination of transmission quality. itu.int/rec/T-REC-P.800. Definition of the Mean Opinion Score used in the codec table.
