Before you send this for real

Ask every carrier for the same three things and the quotes become comparable: the price per channel per month, the price per minute for calls outside the bundle, and the notice period to cancel. Vendors rarely present all three in the same place, because the cheap headline number is usually funded by one of the other two.

Ask also whether the channel count is a hard cap or a burstable one. A hard cap gives busy signals; a burstable trunk gives a surprise invoice. Which of those you prefer is a real decision, and it is better made before the contract than after.
